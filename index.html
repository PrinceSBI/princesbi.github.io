About Me

Technical Lead - Structures Department, R&D @ Solar Defence and Aerospace

I am Shreyas G S, an aerospace engineer with a Master's degree in Aerospace Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay). I specialize in structural analysis, Finite Element Analysis (FEA), computational mechanics, and simulation-driven design, with a strong foundation in structural mechanics, numerical methods, and engineering problem-solving.

Currently, I work in the R&D Structures division at Solar Defence and Aerospace, contributing to the structural design, analysis, and validation of UAVs for defence applications. My experience includes evaluating airframes and launch canisters under demanding operational conditions, including high-acceleration launch loads, transient shock, deployment dynamics, and flight loads. Using ANSYS Workbench and ACP, I perform static, dynamic, and modal analyses, evaluate composite structures, investigate stress concentrations and potential failure modes, and validate simulation results through analytical calculations. I also contribute to structural design iterations, preliminary sizing, and the development of Python tools for engineering calculations and load estimation.

Previously, as an R&D Verification Engineer at Ansys, I worked on verifying finite element formulations and validating simulation results against benchmark solutions across multiple analysis types and material models. This experience strengthened my understanding of solver behavior, numerical accuracy, and the fundamentals underlying reliable FEA. At BlueHat Solutions, I performed linear and nonlinear structural analysis of aerospace components, modal and frequency response analysis, critical load calculations, and post-flight analysis. I also developed automated workflows for aerodynamic load mapping, weight and centre-of-gravity calculations, and structural assessment.

During my M.Tech at IIT Bombay, I worked on an MTech project were I researched Machine Learning–Finite Element Method (ML-FEM) hybrid models for nonlinear constitutive modeling. I also developed a Python-based FEM solver for one-dimensional bar, beam, and truss elements, implementing static and modal analysis and validating numerical results against analytical solutions. These projects deepened my understanding of finite element formulations, numerical computation, and the relationship between theoretical mechanics and practical engineering analysis.

Beyond my technical experience, I served as a Senior Design Engineer with Team SHUNYA at IIT Bombay, contributing to Project Vivaan, a net-zero, solar-powered house developed for the Solar Decathlon competition. This experience strengthened my ability to collaborate across disciplines and translate engineering concepts into practical solutions.

I am passionate about aerospace structures, structural integrity, advanced FEA, composite structures, and simulation-driven design. My goal is to develop safe, lightweight, and reliable engineering solutions by combining strong fundamentals in structural mechanics with rigorous analysis, engineering judgment, and computational tools.
